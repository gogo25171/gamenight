# :knife: Mongolpuri

**4–15 players — social deduction.**

Players are secretly assigned a role. The village must find the Killer before
the Killer runs out of victims.

## Roles

| Role | Goal | At night |
|------|------|----------|
| **Killer** | Reduce the living players to two | Chooses a victim |
| **Doctor** | Keep the village alive | Chooses someone to protect |
| **Villager** | Vote out the Killer | Confirms they are awake |

In larger games there is roughly **one Doctor per five players**, to keep things
balanced.

Roles are hidden by default — tap your role card to reveal it. That way you can
check your own role without flashing it at whoever is sitting next to you.

## A round

=== "Night"

    Every player acts: villagers tap **I'm awake**, the Killer picks a target,
    the Doctor picks someone to protect. The screen looks identical for every
    role, so nobody can tell who is choosing and who is just tapping.

    Once everyone has acted, the server waits a random delay before resolving —
    otherwise the timing itself would give away how many players had a real
    decision to make.

=== "Day"

    Dawn reveals what happened. If the Doctor protected the Killer's target,
    nobody dies. The village then debates during a timed discussion.

=== "Vote"

    Everyone votes for a suspect. The player with the most votes is eliminated
    and their role is revealed. Eliminated players become spectators.

## Winning

- **Villagers win** by voting out the Killer.
- **The Killer wins** by reducing the living players to two.

## Settings

| Setting | Options |
|---------|---------|
| Discussion time | 1 min · 90 s · **2 min** · 2.5 min · 3 min |
| Voting time | 30 s · 45 s · **60 s** · 90 s |

## Tips

- Silence is suspicious, but so is talking too much. The Killer often leads the
  accusations.
- If you are the Doctor, protecting yourself works — but only once or twice
  before people notice nobody is dying.
- Losing a player to a disconnect counts as a death. If too few players remain,
  the game ends as abandoned.
