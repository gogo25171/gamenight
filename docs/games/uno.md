# :black_joker: UNO

**2+ players — classic card game.**

Standard UNO rules. Each player starts with seven cards. Empty your hand first
to win.

## Playing

On your turn, play a card that matches the top of the discard pile by **colour**
or by **value**, or draw one from the deck.

## Special cards

| Card | Effect |
|------|--------|
| **Skip** | The next player loses their turn |
| **Reverse** | Play changes direction |
| **+2** | The next player draws two cards and loses their turn |
| **Wild** | You choose the active colour |
| **Wild +4** | You choose the colour and the next player draws four |

## Settings

None — UNO plays by standard rules.

## Notes

- Your name is shown in the top-left of the game header, so you always know
  which device is yours when several are lying on the table.
- If a player disconnects, their hand is removed and play continues. When only
  one player is left, they win by default.
- Refreshing restores your hand exactly as it was.
