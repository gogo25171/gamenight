# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

GameNight exists to make evenings with friends more fun. We want contributing to
it to feel the same way. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience,
background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good faith — most disagreements are misunderstandings
- Give feedback on the code, not on the person who wrote it
- Accept that maintainers may say no to a feature, and that "no" is not personal
- Remember that most people here are volunteers doing this for fun

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or imagery
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions or deliberately derailing threads

The project also ships user-facing content — word lists, quiz questions,
avatars. Contributions of slurs or hateful content fall under this section.

## Reporting

If you see behaviour that breaks these rules, contact the maintainer privately
through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that
break this code of conduct, and may block repeat offenders from the repository.
